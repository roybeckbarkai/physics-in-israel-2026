Program

(Preliminary) Scientific
Program Highlights

The symposium is designed to bring together physicists from across Israel and abroad for a full day of scientific exchange, networking, and career development.

  1. /01

    Plenary Lecture

  2. /02

    Research Highlights Across Physics

    Invited talks from leading researchers representing a broad spectrum of physics, including particle physics, condensed matter, astrophysics, quantum science, optics, biophysics, and other fields.

  3. /03

    Rising Stars in Physics

    A dedicated session featuring outstanding postdoctoral researchers who are seeking faculty positions. Selected speakers will have the opportunity to present their research to the broader physics community and to representatives of Israeli universities. This session aims to increase the visibility of exceptional early-career scientists while providing departments with an opportunity to meet promising future faculty members.

  4. /04

    Physics & AI

    A special session discussing the rapidly evolving intersection of physics and AI. How is AI changing the way we do physics, and how can physics help shape the next generation of AI? The session will showcase emerging applications, opportunities, and challenges across both fields.

  5. /05

    Academic Opportunities Panel

    Heads of physics departments and schools will discuss current and future faculty opportunities, strategic directions, and how to prepare for job interviews.

  6. /06

    Discussion Session: Maintaining International Scientific Collaborations in Challenging Times

    How to maintain international collaborations, navigate the effects of academic boycotts, participate in conferences and research visits abroad, build successful international partnerships, and share experiences and practical strategies from members of the community.

  7. /07

    Students and Postdoc Poster Session

  8. /08

    Awards
