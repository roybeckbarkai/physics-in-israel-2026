Global Connections Meetup

Global Connections:

Connecting Israeli Academia with the World

Monday, October 26, 2026 · 17:00–18:30 · Tel Aviv University · As part of the Mind-IL conferences
17:00–17:30 · Reception, with wine, cheese and networking
  • Would you like to help build academic ties between your institution and universities in Israel? For example, setting up joint research centers, creating postdoctoral programs for Israelis, or bringing researchers from abroad to visit Israel.
  • The Israel Young Academy invites senior faculty and Young Academy members, both abroad and in Israel, to a focused brainstorming session on building international academic collaborations with Israel and strengthening existing ones.
  • Join the vice presidents for international affairs of Israeli universities, the founders of the Kalaniyot program, and the head of academic excellence at Yad Hanadiv for a first, face-to-face conversation about practical ways to advance academic partnerships.
  • And most important: an open discussion with the audience, aimed at developing concrete, creative and effective ways to connect researchers, institutions and academic communities around the world with universities in Israel.
