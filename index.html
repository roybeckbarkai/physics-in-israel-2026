One day. The whole community.

A scientific symposium bringing together the Israeli physics community — researchers based at universities and institutes in Israel, alongside colleagues working at institutions abroad who happen to be in the country during this period.

The meeting offers an opportunity to share recent results, identify new collaborations, and strengthen scientific ties across the field — both within the local community and beyond it.

The physics symposium is part of a broader initiative to hold community-oriented scientific gatherings across different fields. To help shape the program and the distribution of sub-fields, we invite you to register. If you would like to take part in organizing the event, you are warmly invited to contact the committee members.
